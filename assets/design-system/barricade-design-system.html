Board geometry
9×9 cells, no gap. Separators are 1px inset shadows at 30% wood/700. Barricades are 2 cells long, 1.5cqw thick, centred on the cell boundary. Goal rows carry a tinted band plus a 3px edge line so the objective reads without color.
HUD safe areas
Board occupies the centre 38% of width and is never overlapped. Player rails sit in the outer 20cqw columns, action bar in the bottom 8cqw, turn sign in the top 6cqw. On mobile the rails stack above and the action bar below.

How to play
instruction-manual cards · mini board diagrams
1
Move one square
On your turn, step one square up, down, left or right. No diagonals.
2
Place barricades
Spend a barricade instead of moving. It spans two squares and blocks both sides.
3
Never seal a path
NOT ALLOWED
A barricade is illegal if it leaves any player with no route to their goal.
4
Reach your goal
Touch any square on the opposite edge and the match is yours.

Export as separate layers
Ship every button as a 9-sliced wood plate (no baked text) plus a Roblox TextLabel. Slice insets: 18px all sides at 1x, 36px at 2x. Tokens, barricades and icons export as transparent PNG at 1x/2x. Wood grain lives in one tileable 256px texture reused at three tints.
Animation timings
Button hover 80ms ease-out (−2px), press 60ms (+5px), release 100ms. Token active bob 1.6s loop, ±7px. Barricade drop 180ms ease-in with a 40ms 4% squash on landing. Victory panel scale-in 140ms, confetti burst 900ms. Menu transitions fade 120ms + 12px slide.
Consistency rules
Light always from above. Top highlight rgba(255,255,255,.4), bottom inset rgba(0,0,0,.18), hard bottom edge in the fill's −30% shade. Radii: 20 panel, 16 button, 12 card, 10 chip, 999 pill. No outlines except the 4px #FFD666 selection ring. Saturation stays below the player colors for every neutral.
